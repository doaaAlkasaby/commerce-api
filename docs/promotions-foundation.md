## Configuration and lifecycle

Definitions use versioned, Zod-validated configurations with bounded groups, explicit templates and one reward shape. The supported shapes cover Buy X Get Y, multi-item and multi-quantity all-required conditions, free products, cross-product discounts, fixed-price and discounted bundles, Spend & Get, Mix & Match, selected gifts, tiered quantities and free shipping. A selector references a product, a specific variant with its parent product, a category or a brand. Free and selected gifts use explicit sellable selectors. Campaign and promotion presentation require Arabic and English titles, with optional descriptions and imagery.

Lifecycle uses stored merchant intent and server time. Drafts never evaluate as active. Published records derive scheduled or active status from their start; expiration takes precedence over pause. The start is inclusive and the end is exclusive. Persist absolute timestamps and use the tenant timezone to interpret merchant-entered local wall times at the future request boundary. Publish locks financial terms, targeting and schedule even when the start is in the future. Duplicate a definition into a fresh draft for material changes. A campaign's published window governs discovery; associated promotions retain independent eligibility clocks.

## Pure evaluation

Call `preparePromotionDefinition` with a tenant-scoped repository record to validate stored JSON and rebuild its selector and audience data. `evaluatePromotions` indexes trusted caller-supplied facts through `preparePromotionContext`. The engine consumes a backend timestamp, currency and precision, original paid lines with resolved per-unit prices and tax-inclusive merchandise gross, canonical reward sellables, verified customer facts, usage headroom, coupon state, a single fulfillment source snapshot, and an eligible shipping charge when available. Gift keys and optional Mix & Match selector keys are customer requests validated against the definition. The caller supplies all prices, stock and eligibility facts. No evaluation queries or writes the database.

| Template                   | Evaluation behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Buy X Get Y / all-required | Complete groups can include different quantities per selector. Explicit free, percentage and fixed rewards can name one or several sellables.                                                                                                                             |
| Cross-product              | Explicit rewards consume separate sellable quantities. Existing amount/percentage configurations with selector lists discount the remaining matching cart units and require one reward unit per application.                                                              |
| Bundle                     | Fixed price, fixed discount or percentage across complete product groups. Fixed prices and discounts are capped by the eligible amount.                                                                                                                                   |
| Spend & Get                | Uses anchored pre-adjustment gross merchandise including applicable tax and excludes shipping and fees. Cart units assigned as separate product rewards are removed from threshold spend. Supports merchandise discounts, free products, gift choice and shipping reward. |
| Mix & Match                | Union of configured product, variant, category and brand selectors. An optional validated subset narrows matching. Includes free products, percentage, fixed discount and fixed bundle price.                                                                             |
| Choose Your Gift           | Requires a valid promotion-scoped option, canonical sellable identity and configured quantity per application. Returns remaining viable options when the original choice fails.                                                                                           |
| Tiered quantity            | Highest matching tier applies once. Current and next thresholds are available for guidance.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Free shipping              | Uses the supplied eligible shipping charge once per order. An unavailable charge produces a conditional benefit without a monetary estimate.                                                                                                                              |

Stored version 1 configurations remain valid. Optional `stacking: EXCLUSIVE` and the `PRODUCT_REWARDS` group extend the existing JSON schema. The default stacking policy allows independent offers on disjoint units. A valid explicit coupon takes precedence. Unknown coupon status or missing configured usage headroom holds otherwise eligible offers pending confirmation.

### Unit and monetary accounting

The ledger tracks remaining paid capacity by cart line and proposed capacity by variant. Capacitated matching gives each line a shared quantity budget, including when broad and narrow selectors overlap. A physical unit cannot serve as both a separate qualifier and a product reward. A bundle or tier discounts its own qualifying units. Proposed units come from the single supplied fulfillment source, after subtracting original paid quantities and previously proposed rewards. They never become qualifiers. An absent stock entry leaves any required new unit provisional. Known unavailable stock suppresses the reward and preserves ordinary paid lines.

The engine ranks by descending priority, savings and ascending promotion ID. Ranking considers the reference value of new free items. Confirmed merchandise savings count discounts on existing lines only. A free item newly added to the order carries its own discount evidence and tax class, and its eventual tax liability belongs to the shared tax service. Confirmed shipping savings are tracked separately. Rates round half-up on the applicable aggregate, then the shared largest-remainder allocator distributes exact minor units with stable line-ID ties. Fixed discounts apply per complete group and are capped by the eligible base. These savings are confirmed against the supplied snapshot and remain advisory for checkout.

Repeat counts are found by binary search over capacity. The matching graph represents quantities by line and requirement with up to two tranches for fractional per-unit spend shares. It never expands physical units. After applying an offer, only pending candidates sharing its line or stock dependencies are recalculated. Priority resolution is deterministic and can differ from globally optimal savings across all promotion combinations.

### Customer segments and guidance

- `ALL` includes anonymous customers when the offer has no customer-specific requirement.
- `FIRST_TIME` means a verified tenant customer with no qualifying completed standard order. `RETURNING` requires at least one. A qualifying prior order is completed and paid in the tenant. The caller owns the authoritative history query and applicable refund classification. Anonymous or missing history cannot establish first-time or returning status.
- `SELECTED` matches the explicit active tenant customer identity. Customer eligibility failures, inaccessible offers and inactive windows are suppressed from recommendations.

The result keeps internal allocations and rejection diagnostics distinct from structured guidance. Stable reason codes carry missing selectors or quantities, spend shortfalls, eligible and provisional gift choices, invalidated choices, next tiers, additional bundle opportunities, unavailable rewards and conditional shipping. A future customer API must project the customer guidance fields and omit internal eligibility diagnostics. Remaining units and proposal quantities allow checkout to repeat validation against fresh facts.

## Checkout and accounting contract for the next phases

1. Resolve catalog sale prices and original merchandise tax first. Spend thresholds use eligible sale-priced merchandise gross of applicable merchandise tax. For inclusive pricing use the line gross as stored in the calculation, and for exclusive pricing add the pre-adjustment merchandise tax once. Exclude fees, shipping, reward units and Special Orders. Never feed promotion output back into eligibility.
2. Propose applications with stable condition and reward unit identities. Resolve conflicts by descending configured priority, then greater savings, then stable promotion ID. Consume qualifier and rewarded units once across conflicting applications. Default to one application per promotion per order and reject same-order coupon stacking until a policy can preserve explicit coupon semantics. A valid explicit coupon takes precedence by default. Unit-based promotions may opt into bounded repeats. Free shipping adjusts only the selected eligible fee.
3. Allocate merchandise discounts in existing currency minor units with capped bases and stable largest-remainder tie breakers. Reuse the allocation math in `common/tax/tax.allocation.ts` with unique line keys. Preserve SKU and price snapshots and recalculate taxes using existing rules. Confirm jurisdiction-specific gift taxable bases before rollout. Keep paid and free order lines financially distinct. Sum their quantities by inventory identity for allocation and reserve stock in the existing transaction. Publishing does not reserve stock.
4. Write promotion and item evidence in the checkout transaction. Acquire promotion locks in stable ID order after existing checkout-scope locks. Check and consume active applications inside the same transaction. Use the same lock order for releases that affect usage limits. Unique order/promotion redemption identity makes replays safe. Persist the validated definition, currency, allocations and application evidence at checkout; a later cancellation may release usage without deleting evidence.
5. Refund from immutable line allocations and the existing payment ledger. Use a distinct policy for lost qualification on partial returns and any required gift returns. Resolve tax and fulfillment effects before issuing refunds. Further customer charges require a separately approved settlement policy.

The future fact loader supplies tenant-scoped active membership and qualifying order history. Campaign discovery and notifications should resolve tenant visibility, current lifecycle and audience before dispatch. Aggregate redemption and allocation evidence for savings and usage analytics. Sales and inventory can support transparent rule-based suggestions.

## Persistence and deployment

`PromotionCampaign` owns presentation and visibility. `Promotion` owns terms, limits and a versioned configuration which refers to selector keys. `PromotionSelector` and `PromotionCustomer` bind those definitions to catalog and tenant membership rows. `PromotionRedemption` and `PromotionAllocation` link immutable financial evidence to standard orders and their lines. PostgreSQL preserves published terms and immutable evidence. It allows a one-time release timestamp on redemption.

Catalog records and selected memberships referenced by definitions are protected by foreign keys. Before exposing definition management, coordinate catalog retirement and membership cleanup with those retention rules. The migration creates composite unique indexes on existing catalog and order tables for shared keys. Review ordinary index creation lock and scan costs against production table sizes before deployment.

## PR.3 commerce integration status

The cart guidance increment is additive and disabled by default. The server can enable it for specific tenant IDs using `PROMOTION_CART_GUIDANCE_TENANT_IDS` in an isolated environment. The allowlist has no effect on checkout or payable discounts. Existing cart reads and successful ordinary cart mutations return the same paid items, quantities, and subtotal. Enabled responses also include `promotionGuidance`, with status `ADVISORY`, currency, potential merchandise savings, evaluated offers and actionable recommendations. The API returns guidance through the existing cart routes. It does not insert derived reward items into the paid item array. A candidate set beyond the 50-definition evaluation cap returns `CAPACITY_EXCEEDED` instead of ranking a partial set. Offers, recommendations, and nested option lists each have a 15-entry response limit.

The cart maps existing sale prices at one timestamp and passes tenant-owned lines to the batched commerce preparation service. That service loads active definitions with selector and audience records, confirms active membership, reads completed paid history when needed, aggregates unreleased usage by promotion, and loads only reward sellables covered by the definitions. It accepts trusted destination tax, fulfillment, coupon and shipping facts for future checkout integration. The existing cart has no persisted coupon choice or destination context. Addressless cart guidance omits spend conditions and leaves new reward stock and shipping benefits provisional. Checkout still owns coupon validation.

### Required integration decisions and remaining work

- Merchandise tax currently resolves a tax profile and class rule against a destination. The schema and tax service express ordinary taxable bases, inclusive and exclusive rates, and zero-rated and exempt treatments. They do not express whether an added promotional gift is taxable at reference value, which party bears that tax, or when customer confirmation is required. The checkout shipping path adds the eligible fee separately from merchandise tax. It has no shipping tax category or rule to recalculate after a shipping benefit. Gift-derived charges and free-shipping settlement require a representable policy before those offers change a payable total.
- Gift selection and decline persistence, add-entire-offer, and final cart discounts still require implementation. The existing paid cart item identity stays intact.
- Checkout preview is read-only and returns an estimate. There is no accepted-preview binding in the current confirmation contract. PR.3B still needs material-change validation, a single feasible paid-and-reward inventory plan, transactional usage limits, and order allocation evidence. Capacity shown in cart guidance is advisory.
- The regular order lifecycle supports full cancellation and COD refusal or warehouse return. The refund ledger tracks payment capacity and provider recovery. There is no general regular-order item-return quantity workflow, approval contract, or settlement policy for retained promotional entitlement. PR.3C still needs that foundation for partial returns and sequential entitlement recovery. Special Orders has separate contracts and remains outside scope.
- PR.4 through PR.7 remain downstream of commerce completion. Merchant configuration and client adoption, customer discovery, notifications, analytics, and additional campaign workflows are independent of this advisory cart response.
